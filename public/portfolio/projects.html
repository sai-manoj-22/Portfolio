Project overview

Intelli-Helmet is a smart helmet that brings sensor-based safety checks to two-wheeler riding. It verifies that the rider is wearing the helmet, checks for alcohol, and watches for signs of drowsiness — alerting the rider when needed.

Problem statement

Riding without a helmet, riding under the influence of alcohol and rider fatigue are risk factors in two-wheeler road accidents. A conventional helmet cannot detect or prevent any of these conditions.

Objectives

  • Ensure the vehicle starts only when the helmet is worn.
  • Prevent the bike from starting if alcohol is detected.
  • Identify signs of rider fatigue by tracking eye movement.
  • Alert the rider with a beep to keep them awake.

Features

Helmet-wearing detection

The vehicle starts only when the helmet is worn.

Alcohol detection

Prevents the bike from starting if alcohol is detected.

Drowsiness detection

Tracks eye movement to identify signs of fatigue.

Alert mechanism

Triggers an alert beep to keep the rider awake.

System functionality

The safety logic runs as a sequence of checks before and during the ride:

Helmet worn?→Alcohol detected?→Vehicle can start→Monitor eye movement→Fatigue → alert beep

  1. Helmet check

    If the helmet is not worn, the vehicle does not start.

  2. Alcohol check

    If alcohol is detected, the bike is prevented from starting.

  3. Drowsiness monitoring

    While riding, eye movement is tracked to identify signs of fatigue.

  4. Alert

    When fatigue is detected, an alert beep is triggered to keep the rider awake.

Implementation details

The project combines embedded sensor integration with real-time safety logic. Sensors integrated into the helmet provide helmet-wearing, alcohol and eye-movement inputs, and the safety logic uses these inputs to control vehicle start-up and trigger the alert mechanism.

Project outcome

A working smart helmet that combines four safety mechanisms — helmet-wearing detection, alcohol detection, drowsiness detection and an alert mechanism — aimed at reducing road accident risks for two-wheeler riders.
